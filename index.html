Five roles, one person

What I handle

Most projects split these across several people. I usually do all of them on the same piece of work.

The client

I'm in contact with clients every day. I take their requests, work out with them what we will build, and turn that into tasks the team can start on.

Planning

Sprint planning, estimates and priorities. I keep the client up to date on where the work stands and what is coming next.

The team

I have led teams of three to seven engineers: handing out work, reviewing pull requests, mentoring junior developers and helping with hiring interviews.

Code

I still write code, usually the parts that are hardest to get right: integrations, messaging between services, and moving live systems to newer versions of .NET.

AI

I use Claude Code for most of my day-to-day work, and Codex alongside it, to get client requests built and shipped faster.

I have not held formal line-management responsibility, and my cloud work has been on Azure rather than AWS.
