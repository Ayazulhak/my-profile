Selected work

Fleet tracking and staff scheduling

Cybersoft · 2022–23 · client design sessions, architecture, team lead

Several tracking-device providers sent location pings in their own formats and at their own intervals. I led the design of a pipeline on Azure Service Bus and Azure Functions that applied the client's scheduling rules to all of them, about 20,000 pings a day.

Health information systems for the Saudi Ministry of Health

National Technology Group · 2016–20 · development, on-site deployment, staff training, sprint planning

I built health information systems, then went on site to deploy them and train the staff who would use them. For one primary health care centre I ran the work from requirements to maintenance, including sprint planning for a team of six.

Splitting an airline integration monolith

Calrom · 2021–22 · development, monitoring, team stand-ups

I helped move multi-tenant airline integration services from a monolith toward separate services, and set up central monitoring with Azure Monitor and Log Analytics. I also took over the team's stand-ups and task priorities.
