The portfolio is also a product

Designing for three readers without turning the site into three websites.

The brief was to make ten years of confidential project work understandable to recruiters, technical interviewers, and professional reviewers.

Information architecture

Three starting routes

A fast case-study route, a technical architecture route, and an evidence route let each visitor choose depth without hiding the rest.

Confidentiality

Function over client identity

Forty-two systems use functional names. Client, repository, and internal product names are deliberately excluded.

Credibility

Pending is a valid status

Unknown contribution boundaries and unverified outcomes are marked pending instead of being converted into polished but unsupported claims.

Interaction

Architecture as a guided story

Each system map supports search, selection, and a step-by-step animated connection flow, with reduced-motion support.

Discoverability

Human and machine-readable

Canonical URLs, structured data, a sitemap, robots directives, and an LLM-readable profile summary describe the same public evidence.

Performance & access

Static, responsive, keyboard-aware

The site uses lightweight HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, a mobile menu, skip links, semantic headings, and print-friendly evidence pages.
