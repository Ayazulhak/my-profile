Telemetry & scheduling

Turning asynchronous device pings into operational events.

Tracking devices from multiple providers emitted pings at different times. The product needed to receive that data, evaluate it against business rules, and make it useful for fleet and staff scheduling.

I served as the main technical lead, staying close to both the architecture and delivery. Azure Service Bus decoupled ingestion from processing; Azure Functions handled the asynchronous workflow.
