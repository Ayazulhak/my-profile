Case 01 · Confirmed

Device telemetry and scheduling platform

Context

A fleet operator receiving location pings from several tracking-device providers. Each provider sent data at its own cadence and in its own shape.

Problem

Pings had to be received, evaluated against business rules and turned into fleet and staff scheduling decisions, without coupling the workflow to any one provider or losing data during provider outages.

What I did

Technical lead for design and delivery. Used Azure Service Bus to decouple ingestion from processing, Azure Functions to apply business rules to the asynchronous pings, and Azure Monitor with Log Analytics for centralized service monitoring.

Outcome

In production at about 20,000 pings a day. No uptime or SLA figure is published because I have not been authorised to share one.

Case 02 · Conservatively worded

Monolith to services in a multi-tenant airline platform

Problem

A shared platform carrying integrations for several airline tenants had grown into a single point of failure with unclear ownership between teams.

What I did

Helped move shared capabilities into independently deployable services with their own data and contracts, so each could be owned and released separately.

Outcome

Fewer single points of failure, clearer ownership boundaries. Tenant, client and service names stay private by agreement.
